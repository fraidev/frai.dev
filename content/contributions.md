Most of my work in the last years landed in other people's repositories. At InfinyOn that meant [Fluvio](https://github.com/fluvio-community/fluvio), a streaming platform in Rust: producer performance, cluster mirroring, connectors, the Python client and the docs. At Deno it was Node.js compatibility inside the [runtime](https://github.com/denoland/deno), from `child_process` to worker debugging, plus deno_core, rusty_v8 and node_shim. Before that, the Tezos ecosystem with Marigold: [TzProxy](https://github.com/marigold-dev/tzproxy), a reverse proxy for Tezos nodes I wrote and maintained, TzStamp, and the Unity and Rust SDKs. And a few drive-by fixes elsewhere: Bun, Zig, workerd, React, piaf, This Week in Rust.
